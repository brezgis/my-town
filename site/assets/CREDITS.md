# Asset credits

Vesper Street is built on the engine of Anna's House (a walk-in bungalow) and shares its sources.
Everything not listed here was drawn or built in code for the street: the shopfronts, signs,
labels, posters, packaging, robots, trees, the sky.

- three.js r170 (vendor/three), MIT licence.
- Textures (assets/tex), all CC0: Poly Haven (polyhaven.com) `grey_roof_01`, `red_brick`,
  `brick_wall_001`, `red_brick_pavers`, `terracotta_floor_tiles`, `oak_wood_planks`,
  `wood_table_001`, `painted_plaster_wall`, `bark_brown_02` and the fabric, stone and ground sets
  described in assets/tex/TEXTURES.md; ambientCG (ambientcg.com) `WoodSiding013`, `Grass004`.
- Models (assets/models), CC0: Poly Haven furniture, food and garden pieces; a few from the
  Smithsonian Open Access collection and the Khronos glTF sample assets. Each is listed with its
  source in assets/models/CATALOG.md (the catalogue covers the house's whole collection; only
  some of it is used here). Textures reduced in size.
- Music (assets/audio): public-domain and CC0 recordings of Satie, Bach, Chopin, Schumann, Mozart
  and Beethoven from Musopen, the Open Goldberg Variations and Open Well-Tempered Clavier
  projects, via Wikimedia Commons; performers and sources are in assets/audio/manifest.json.
  The fire and the birds are CC0 field recordings (same manifest).
- Book titles on the spines (assets/books.json): a list of real books compiled for the house.
- Typefaces (assets/fonts), SIL Open Font Licence 1.1, Latin subsets as served by Google Fonts:
  Sorts Mill Goudy (Barry Schwartz), Playfair Display (Claus Eggers Sørensen), Abril Fatface
  (TypeTogether), Pacifico (Vernon Adams and others), Bebas Neue (Ryoichi Tsunekawa), Caveat
  (Impallari Type), Limelight (Sorkin Type), DM Serif Display (Colophon Foundry), Fraunces
  (Undercase Type), Shrikhand (Jonny Pinhorn), Yellowtail (Astigmatic), Archivo Black (Omnibus-Type).
  Special Elite (Astigmatic) is under the Apache Licence 2.0.
- Every shop, brand, band, book jacket, film and product name on the street is invented.
